## Anomalies

### 1. `bachelor-of-arts-in-architecture.txt` vs. `bachelor-of-arts-degree-and-bachelor-of-science-degrees.txt` — cross-file total conflict
- The general policy page states: *"The Bachelor of Architecture degree requires the completion of at least 181 units, over five years."*
- The dedicated Architecture program page's `Program Structure` table sums cleanly to **151** (31 + 105 + 15), and lists courses only up to a level consistent with a 4-year sequence (course codes ADS 101–ADS 501).
- These are two different numbers (181 vs. 151) for what should be the same fact. The architecture page is also marked "(Teach Out)" in its title, suggesting the program has since been restructured/discontinued and the 151 figure is the current one, while 181 may be a stale figure left on the general overview page. **Trust the dedicated program page (151) over the general overview page (181) if forced to pick one, but flag for human confirmation** — don't silently resolve it in code.

### 2. `bachelor-of-arts-degree-and-bachelor-of-science-degrees.txt` — word/numeral mismatch
- Line 8: *"...at least one hundred twenty four (124) units, of which sixty two (61) are in General Education/Liberal Studies."*
- "sixty two" (word) does not match "(61)" (numeral) — off by one. Can't tell from the text alone which is correct. This file is excluded from the facts table anyway (it's a general policy page, not a specific degree), but flagging in case anyone later mines prose text for this kind of fact.

### 3. `faculty-of-law-bachelor-of-arts-in-law-law-single-degree.txt` — Program Structure table doesn't match the course list below it
- The `Program Structure` summary (lines 15–19) states: Foundation 18, Major Core 27, Major Elective 18, General Elective 18, Gen Ed 31 → sums to **112**, but every row's parenthetical says the total is **(124)**.
- Further down in the same file, the actual course-list section headers (lines 20–61) give **different** numbers for the same categories: "LAW Major Courses (Required: 30 credits)" and "LAW Major Electives Courses (Required: 30 credits)" and "Electives Courses (Required: 15 credits)" — not 27/18/18 as the summary table claims.
- Summing the section headers instead — Gen Ed 31 + Foundation 18 + Major 30 + Major Electives 30 + Electives 15 = **124** — which *does* match the stated total in the summary table's parentheses.
- **Conclusion: the total of 124 is correct and corroborated by the detailed course list; the `Program Structure` summary table's individual category numbers (27, 18, 18) are the buggy part**, not the 124 itself. A regex that only extracts the `(N)` parenthetical for `total_credits` will get the right answer (124) here even though the row-level breakdown is unreliable for this one file.

### 4. `faculty-of-law-master-of-laws-in-artificial-intelligence.txt` — three different totals
- Prose (opening paragraph): *"The program comprises 12 courses and a Capstone project, totaling **36 credits**..."*
- Section heading: `Required Courses (39 Credits)` — this heading covers "Core Courses (24 credits)" + "Major Courses (15 credits)" = 39.
- But the file *also* lists a separate `Elective Courses (6 credits)` section beneath that, which isn't included in the "39." If electives are required, the real total is **45** (24 + 15 + 6).
- So three numbers appear in one file: 36 (prose, doesn't match any table breakdown), 39 (heading, explicitly excludes electives), 45 (full sum if electives count). None of the three sibling LLM files (`cybersecurity`, `international-business...`) mention "36" anywhere, reinforcing that "36" is likely just a copy-paste/typo error unique to this page. This file needs manual resolution, not regex extraction.

### 5. `faculty-of-law-master-of-laws-in-cybersecurity.txt` and `faculty-of-law-master-of-laws-in-international-business-and-digital-technologies.txt` — ambiguous total

### 6. `faculty-of-digital-technologies-master-of-science-in-computer-science-ai-master-of-science-in-ai.txt` and `...-dual-degree.txt` — no total stated at all

## Extraction recommendation

**Files that need zero/one extraction work (28 of 55):** faculty/hub/index pages, staff directories, non-credit certificate courses, and the two form/stub pages have no "total credits" fact to extract at all. Skip them entirely — don't run any regex over them.

**Files extractable by a single deterministic regex (19 of 55):** all files matching the canonical convention:

```regex
Program Structure:\s*(?P<category>[^|]+?)\s*\|\s*Required Total Credits?\s*\((?P<total>\d+)\)\s*:\s*(?P<value>\d+)
```

- Iterate all matches in a file; `total` should be identical across every match in the file — take it once per file for `total_credits`.
- Sum `value` across matches to sanity-check against `total` (this is exactly how Anomaly #3 was caught) — but even where the breakdown sum is wrong, `total` itself is still reliably the number to store, as verified against the underlying course list.
- This covers 19 files: 18 with the literal string `Required Total Credits`, plus 1 (`bachelor-of-science-in-itm-computer-science.txt`) with the singular `Required Total Credit` — hence the `s?` in the regex. Combined with `Source:` (line 1 of every file) for `source_url` and the H1-style title line (line 3, after `Source:` + a blank line) for `program_name`, that's a clean `(program_name, total_credits, source_url)` triple for all 19.

**Files extractable by a second, different regex (2 of 55):** the MBA single/dual pages use `(?:Major Courses|Dual MBA Curriculum) \(Required: (?P<total>\d+) credits?\)` at the section-heading level rather than a repeated table row. Simple to add as a second pattern, but it's a genuinely different shape — don't try to force one regex to cover both.

**Files needing manual/semi-manual handling (6 of 55):** the 3 pattern-(d) no-total files (`master-of-legal-studies`, `master-of-science-in-ai`, `...-dual-degree`), the 1 pattern-(e) conflicting-totals file (`master-of-laws-in-artificial-intelligence`), and the 2 pattern-(c) ambiguous "39-vs-45" LLM files (`master-of-laws-in-cybersecurity`, `master-of-laws-in-international-business-and-digital-technologies`). Six files is a small enough number that hand-entering these six `(program_name, total_credits, source_url)` rows after a human decides the ambiguous cases (electives required or not? which of 36/39/45 is right?) is clearly more reliable than trying to write regex heuristics for edge cases this inconsistent — the source data itself is contradictory, not just irregularly formatted.

**Bottom line:** of the ~55 files, **21 can be extracted by two small, deterministic regexes** (19 + 2), **6 need manual/human-in-the-loop handling** because the source text is genuinely ambiguous or self-contradictory (not just differently formatted), and the remaining **28 aren't degree program pages at all** and should be filtered out before extraction runs (e.g., by checking the file isn't in a known hub/staff/certificate exclude-list, or simply by checking whether any credit pattern matches at all and routing "no match" files to a manual-review pile rather than assuming they're missing data).
